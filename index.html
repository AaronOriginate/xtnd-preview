Who We Are

Four people. Two schools. One community.

We're not a big organisation. We're four people — Tamara, Alice, Kate and Cheryl — based right here on the estate.

XTND - Improving Futures is a registered charity (no. 1141966) that does one thing well: we bring families together. We work inside both primary schools, which means we're already part of the local community.

  • Based at Bournville Primary and Windwhistle Primary since 2011
  • Known faces at the school gate every day
  • Free groups, classes and support for families
