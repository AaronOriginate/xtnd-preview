Find Us

Where to Find the XTND Hut

Our main base is at Bournville Primary School on the Bournville estate in Weston-super-Mare, North Somerset. That's where the XTND Hut is. If you've dropped your children at Bournville Primary, you've walked past it.

We also run groups and sessions at Windwhistle Primary School.

Getting here

The Hut is on the school grounds at Bournville Primary. Come in through the main gate and you'll see it. If you're not sure, ask at reception -- or just look for Tamara.

When to Find Us

Best Times to Catch Us

We're around during school hours, Monday to Friday. Drop-off and pick-up times are when you're most likely to bump into someone from the team.

If you want to come to a specific group or session, check our What's On page for times and dates.

And if you just want a chat? That works too. No appointment needed.

Not Sure Where to Start?

That's completely fine. Most people who come to XTND started with a conversation at the school gate. Just say hello. We'll take it from there.
