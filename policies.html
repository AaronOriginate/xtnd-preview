Our Policies

We take safeguarding and good practice seriously. Always have. Here you'll find all our policy documents available to download.

XTND - Improving Futures is a registered charity (no. 1141966) working with families and children in Bournville, Weston-super-Mare. We work inside two primary schools every day, so getting our policies right isn't just paperwork. It's essential.

All our policies are reviewed regularly by our trustees. If you have any questions about them, please get in touch.
