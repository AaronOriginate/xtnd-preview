Our Story
Our Story
It started in 2011

It started at two primary schools on the Bournville estate in Weston-super-Mare. The idea wasn’t complicated. Families on the estate needed connection, not another service with forms and waiting lists.

So XTND set up inside the schools. Literally inside them. We got a base at Bournville Primary School (the XTND Hut, as everyone calls it) and started running groups at Windwhistle Primary School too.

We’re a registered charity (no. 1141966) but that’s not how most people know us. They know Tamara at the school gate. They know the Tuesday group. They know the Hut.

Quote Callout
Parents chatting at drop-off started coming to a cooking class. Then a craft session. Then they’d bring a friend. Before long, friendships formed that had nothing to do with us. That’s the bit we’re proudest of, honestly.

— The connections that carry on without any help from XTND at all.

Our Approach
Our Approach
We don’t wait for families to come to us.

Here’s what makes XTND different from most organisations working in the local community. We’re already there.

Already There

Our staff are part of school life at Bournville Primary and Windwhistle Primary. They’re at drop-off. They’re at pick-up. They know the children’s names.

Natural Support

Families who’d never walk through the door of a formal service will happily come to a cooking class run by someone they already know. The support happens naturally. That’s by design.

Connected Network

We run groups. We run classes. We connect people. And when someone needs more than we can offer, we know exactly who to call.

The Bournville Estate
The Bournville Estate
This is home.

Bournville is a small estate on the edge of Weston-super-Mare in North Somerset. It’s tight-knit. People look out for each other.

It’s the kind of place where people know their neighbours. The mum who organised a street party last summer. The dad who volunteers at the breakfast club every morning. That community spirit is what XTND was built around.

XTND exists because this community is worth investing in. Not from the outside, parachuting in with programmes and then leaving. From the inside, as part of everyday school life, for as long as we’re needed.

  • Part of the Pride in Place regeneration programme (2025–2035)
  • XTND has been here since before that programme existed
  • We’ll be here long after

Introduction
The Team
Here’s who we are

XTND isn’t a big organisation. There’s no head office, no switchboard, no receptionist.

There’s us. Four people who turn up every day because we care about families in the local community. We’re based at Bournville Primary School and Windwhistle Primary School, so you’ll find us where you’d expect — at the gate, in the Hut, setting up for the next group.

Team Bios

Tamara Moody

Operations Lead

Tamara keeps everything running. The groups, the schedules, the partnerships, the funding applications (all of it). She’s the person you’re most likely to bump into at Bournville Primary’s school gate.

If you’ve ever been to a cooking class or a craft session at the Hut, Tamara probably set it up. Ask her about XTND and she’ll tell you about the mum who came to one session and ended up making three friends she still sees every week.

That’s what gets her out of bed in the morning.

Alice

Trustee & Leadership

Alice is the driving force behind XTND’s direction. As a trustee with links to ELAN Trust (the multi-academy trust running both schools), she bridges the gap between the charity and the school community.

She believes families should know who they’re dealing with. Not a logo. Not a charity number. Real people.

Alice cares about the details. Whether the website feels like XTND actually feels when you walk through the door. If it doesn’t feel right, she’ll say so.

Kate

Team Member

Kate is part of the team that makes XTND’s groups and sessions happen on the ground. She’s one of the faces families see regularly at the schools, and that consistency matters.

When children see the same people week after week, trust builds. Not through grand gestures. Through showing up. Kate shows up.

Cheryl

Team Member

Cheryl brings warmth and reliability to everything she does with XTND. She’s part of the reason families come back, session after session.

People don’t come to our groups because of a leaflet. They come because someone like Cheryl made them feel welcome the first time.

For Funders and Partners

XTND – Improving Futures has been embedded in the Bournville estate since 2011. We work inside two primary schools, building trust with families that other organisations struggle to reach.

This page sets out what we do, how we’re funded, and why it works.

The Context
The Context
Understanding Bournville

The Bournville estate in Weston-super-Mare sits within North Somerset 021C — ranked in the top 1% most deprived areas nationally (86th out of approximately 33,000 neighbourhoods in England).

60%+

£34,100

These families don’t lack potential. They lack connection, opportunity, and trust in the services designed to help them. That’s where XTND comes in.

Our Approach
Our Approach
Inside the Schools, Not Outside Them

We don’t operate from an office. We’re physically inside Bournville Primary School and Windwhistle Primary School, running groups from the XTND Hut and school spaces.

Why does this matter? Because our staff — Tamara, Alice, Kate and Cheryl — are known faces at the school gate. Parents don’t “access a service.” They see Tamara at drop-off, get chatting, and end up at a cooking class. The barrier to entry is close to zero.

This is intentional. Our work is often implicit. Families enjoy the group. They make friends. They build confidence. And they don’t always see the wider impact it’s had on their lives. But the outcomes are real.

“People create social networks from our groups, receive the support from other organisations they are signposted to, and children have a more secure childhood.”
Our Impact
Our Impact
Outcomes Across Three Areas

Family Connection

We run cooking classes, parent groups, craft sessions, and family activities across both schools. Families who attend build social networks that extend well beyond our sessions. Friendships form. Isolation reduces. Parents support each other.

Early Support

Our staff have strong relationships with families and children on the estate. They’re fully integrated in the schools, which means they’re well placed to reach the children and families that most need it — before issues escalate. We connect families to partner organisations including Home-Start North Somerset, Public Health, and Horizon Health Centre.

Community Resilience

In 15 years of operation, XTND has become part of the fabric of the Bournville estate. We’re not parachuted in. We live and work here. That continuity matters in an area where trust takes time to build and services come and go.

How We’re Funded
How We’re Funded
Small Charity, Big Impact

XTND - Improving Futures is a registered charity (no. 1141966). Our annual income for 2024/25 was £61,641.

We’re a small team. Every pound goes directly into delivering groups, sessions, and support for families on the estate. There’s no head office, no marketing department, no layers of management. Just four staff, two schools, and a community that trusts us.

Pride in Place
The Opportunity
Pride in Place

Bournville is about to change. The Pride in Place programme is bringing £20 million of regeneration investment to the Bournville, Oldmixon, and Coronation estates between 2025 and 2035.

XTND is already here. We’ve been building trust and connection on this estate for 15 years. As new investment arrives, we’re uniquely placed to ensure it reaches the families who need it most — because we already know them.

We’d welcome conversations with funders and partners who want to be part of what comes next.

Why It Matters
Why It Matters
The Place Families Find Each Other

Imagine moving to a new estate and not knowing anyone. The school run is lonely. The weekends are long. You’d love to meet other parents, but there’s nowhere obvious to start.

That’s what it’s like for a lot of families in Bournville.

XTND is the place they find each other. A cooking class on a Tuesday. A craft session before Christmas. A chat with Tamara at the school gate that turns into a friendship with three other mums by half-term.

It sounds small. It isn’t. These connections change how families experience their neighbourhood. Children feel more settled. Parents feel less alone. And the local community gets a little bit stronger every time someone new walks through the door.

What Your Donation Makes Possible
Every Penny Counts

We’re a small charity. Our total annual income is £61,641. There’s no fat to trim — every penny goes into running sessions and supporting families. Here’s what donations actually pay for:
