Meet the Team

You've probably seen us at the school gate.

We're a small team. Four people who know the schools, know the estate, and probably already know your children's names.

The Team

Here's who we are

XTND isn't a big organisation. There's no head office, no switchboard, no receptionist.

There's us. Four people who turn up every day because we care about families in the local community. We're based at Bournville Primary School and Windwhistle Primary School, so you'll find us where you'd expect -- at the gate, in the Hut, setting up for the next group.

Tamara Moody
Operations Lead
Tamara Moody
Operations Lead

Tamara keeps everything running. The groups, the schedules, the partnerships, the funding applications (all of it). She's the person you're most likely to bump into at Bournville Primary's school gate.

If you've ever been to a cooking class or a craft session at the Hut, Tamara probably set it up. Ask her about XTND and she'll tell you about the mum who came to one session and ended up making three friends she still sees every week.

That's what gets her out of bed in the morning.

Alice
Trustee & Leadership
Alice
Trustee & Leadership

Alice is the driving force behind XTND's direction. As a trustee with links to ELAN Trust (the multi-academy trust running both schools), she bridges the gap between the charity and the school community.

She believes families should know who they're dealing with. Not a logo. Not a charity number. Real people.

Alice cares about the details. Whether the website feels like XTND actually feels when you walk through the door. If it doesn't feel right, she'll say so.

Kate
Team Member
Kate
Team Member

Kate is part of the team that makes XTND's groups and sessions happen on the ground. She's one of the faces families see regularly at the schools, and that consistency matters.

When children see the same people week after week, trust builds. Not through grand gestures. Through showing up. Kate shows up.

Cheryl
Team Member
Cheryl
Team Member

Cheryl brings warmth and reliability to everything she does with XTND. She's part of the reason families come back, session after session.

People don't come to our groups because of a leaflet. They come because someone like Cheryl made them feel welcome the first time.

Want to get involved?

We're always glad to hear from people who want to help out in the local community. Whether that's volunteering, partnering with us, or just coming along to a group -- the door's open.
