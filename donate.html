Why It Matters

The Place Families Find Each Other

Moving somewhere new and not knowing anyone can be isolating. The school run feels quiet. The weekends feel long. You'd love to meet other parents, but there's nowhere obvious to start.

That's what it's like for a lot of families in the local area.

XTND is the place they find each other. A cooking class on a Tuesday. A craft session before Christmas. A chat at the school gate that turns into a friendship with three other parents by half-term.

It might sound small, but these connections change how families experience their community. Children feel more settled. Parents feel less alone. And the local area gets a little bit stronger every time someone new walks through the door.

What Your Donation Makes Possible

Every Penny Counts

We're a small charity. Our total annual income is £61,641. There's no fat to trim -- every penny goes into running sessions and supporting families. Here's what donations actually pay for:

£25

Covers materials for a cooking class -- enough for 8 families to learn a new meal together.

£50

Pays for a craft session at the XTND Hut. Pumpkin carving, Christmas decorations, Easter activities. The things children remember.

£100

Funds a family activity session at one of the schools. Film nights, book swaps, community garden days.

£250

Contributes to a full term of weekly parent group sessions -- where friendships form and families find their feet.

£500

Helps keep our doors open for a month. Staff time, materials, heating the Hut. The basics that make everything else possible.

Where the Money Goes

No Big Overheads. No Fancy Offices.

We're a registered charity (no. 1141966) and we publish our accounts every year. Our 2024/25 annual income was £61,641, and it went directly into:

  • Staff costs for Tamara, Alice, Kate and Cheryl
  • Materials and supplies for groups and classes
  • Running the XTND Hut at Bournville Primary
  • Family activities across both schools and the wider community
  • Signposting and support work with partner organisations

No big overheads. No fancy offices. Just four people doing good work in the heart of the community.
